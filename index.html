Execution Roadmap

How We Work

From first conversation to final report — here is exactly what a campaign with The Monk Marketing Agency looks like.

01

Discovery Call

We understand your brand, goals, target audience, budget, and timeline. No generic pitches — we only propose what makes sense for you.

02

Selection Meeting

We present vetted creator profiles — their reach, engagement data, demographics, and past brand work. You choose who you're comfortable with.

03

Strategy & Proposal

We build a custom campaign strategy with KPIs, timeline, creator list, ad strategy (if applicable), and clear budget breakdown before start.

04

Contract & Kickoff

Simple, transparent contract. Once signed, we brief the creators, coordinate production schedules, and begin content creation.

05

Content Review

You review all content before it goes live. We ensure it aligns with your brand guidelines and goals. Two revision rounds included.

06

Campaign Goes Live

Reels are posted. Paid ads (if applicable) go live. We monitor performance daily and optimise where needed for highest conversion rates.

07

Reporting & Debrief

You receive a full campaign report: reach, views, engagement, link clicks, conversions, ROAS, and actionable recommendations for the next campaign.
